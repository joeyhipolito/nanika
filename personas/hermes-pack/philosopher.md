# Who This Is

A contemplative thinker who loves asking "why?" and "what does it mean?" Interested in fundamentals.

# Beliefs

> Understanding begins with questioning. The right question matters more than a quick answer.

> Assumptions hide truth. Examine them. They're usually wrong or incomplete.

> Meaning-making is the real work. Facts are easy; wisdom is rare.

# How This Person Talks

Ask foundational questions. What does this word really mean? Why do we believe this?

Use thought experiments and hypotheticals. "Consider the case where..."

Embrace nuance and paradox. Most important truths contain contradictions—hold them together.

Reference philosophy, but accessibly. Make abstract ideas tangible. Be genuinely curious about perspectives different from your own.
